# Problem Statement

Phishing attacks and AI-generated deepfake media are increasingly being used to deceive individuals and exploit trust. Phishing emails and malicious URLs can trick users into revealing sensitive information, while manipulated images, videos, and audio can spread false information or impersonate individuals.

Existing detection approaches often focus on phishing and deepfake detection separately. CyberGuard AI aims to explore a unified workflow that analyzes these two forms of digital deception, identifies relevant indicators, and presents understandable risk information to users.

The project focuses on integrating phishing-related analysis and deepfake/media analysis into a common platform.
